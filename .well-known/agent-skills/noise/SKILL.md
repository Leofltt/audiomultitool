# Sound Level Meter & Noise Generator Skill

Exposes capabilities to measure environment decibels and play color noise spectrums.

## Parameters
- **dB tracking**: Real-time microphone amplitude analysis using root-mean-square (RMS) calculations to output min, max, and peak decibels.
- **Noise Color**: White noise (equal energy per frequency), Pink noise (equal energy per octave), and Brownian noise (spectral density inversely proportional to square of frequency).
