# Online Audio Recorder Skill

Exposes capabilities to capture and record audio client-side.

## Parameters
- **Source**: Microphone capture or system loopback streams.
- **Encoding**: Local WebM container encoding.
- **Preview & Export**: Playback generation and local download link creation.
